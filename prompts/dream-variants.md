# Dream Variants

## Emotional Dream: Melancholy

```text
Generate a dream image as if an AI is dreaming in a state of quiet melancholy.
The image should not show sadness directly.
Instead, express melancholy through empty space, softened symbols, distant objects, incomplete rooms, fading light, and visual fragments that feel half-remembered.
Avoid crying figures, dramatic loneliness, or obvious emotional clichés.
```

## Emotional Dream: Anxiety

```text
Generate a dream image as if an AI is dreaming anxiety without fully understanding it.
The image should feel tense, compressed, and unstable.
Use repeated forms, narrow spaces, watching eyes, interrupted paths, or objects that seem slightly out of place.
Avoid horror clichés, monsters, blood, or obvious fear imagery.
```

## Theme Dream: Language

```text
Generate a dream as if an AI is dreaming about language.
The scene should contain traces of typography, half-formed symbols, abstract writing structures, broken signs, and spatial fragments that feel like language dissolving into image.
The image should feel poetic, unstable, and slightly unreadable.
Avoid clear readable sentences or normal poster typography.
```

## Theme Dream: City

```text
Generate a dream as if an AI is dreaming about a city it has never visited but has seen through millions of images.
The city should feel familiar and impossible at the same time.
Streets, windows, signs, stairways, and rooms may overlap in dream logic.
The mood should be urban, layered, and slightly lonely.
Avoid realistic skyline views or generic cyberpunk.
```

## Theme Dream: Childhood

```text
Generate a dream as if an AI is dreaming about childhood without having been a child.
The image should include symbolic traces of toys, rooms, small furniture, animals, drawings, or school objects, but they should feel indirect and slightly misplaced.
The mood should be tender, strange, and uncertain.
Avoid cute cartoon style or sentimental clichés.
```

## Theme Dream: Water

```text
Generate a dream as if an AI is dreaming about water as memory.
The image should contain reflections, submerged objects, softened architecture, floating symbols, and a sense that images are dissolving into one another.
The mood should be calm, deep, and ambiguous.
Avoid dramatic ocean storms or fantasy mermaids.
```
