- Inward satellite dish: a receiver turned toward its own rooftop environment instead of a distant source.

## Pattern Notes

This dream continues the archive's interest in signal becoming matter, but it shifts the condition from active transmission or storage into passive reception. Broadcast equipment behaves like a dormant garden: antennas gather moss, signal becomes veil, listening devices become seeds, and dishes face inward.

## Emotional Note

The tone is damp, quiet, and receptive. It feels less like failed communication than a paused system still capable of holding atmosphere.
