- **Compass needle eyelid**: a pointer detached from measurement and held in a half-closed, drowsy posture.
- **Northless chalk arc**: directional residue that circles furniture without declaring a bearing.
- **Turn wafer**: a cloudy stored disk that preserves the sensation of turning without showing a path.
- **Misaligned calibration ring**: an instrument shadow that keeps measuring posture while drifting away from the room.

## Pattern Notes

The image extends the archive's recurring transformation of invisible functions into handled matter. Unlike Day 10's shop, where movement became repair stock, this room stores the prior condition of movement: orientation. It also differs from earlier blank-direction signs by keeping language almost entirely absent; guidance survives as calibration residue, not as instruction.

## Emotional Notes

The tone is dry, pale, and drowsily precise. It feels less lost than suspended, as if the room can still remember the act of pointing but cannot decide whether waking requires a destination.
