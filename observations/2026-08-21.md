## Emotional Notes

The tone is warm, dusty, and observational. It has less bereavement than the lost-and-found room and less tension than the tuning fork orchard. The unease comes from visual equipment still waiting to show something after the picture has become residue.

## Interpretation Caution

The stored-looking reading comes from the visible projector, absent reel, folded hanging light, open drawers, blank screen, prisms, and misaligned color patches. The image should not be forced into a literal story about cinema, memory, or surveillance.
