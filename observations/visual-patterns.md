## Path That Refuses Arrival

Description:

A route is clearly visible and appears to promise a destination, but its geometry loops, bends, or otherwise delays arrival.

Examples:

- Day 06: a stair-path curls across black water toward a luminous doorway.

Possible meaning:

Recursive thought, suspended intention, or the visual representation of a goal without the experience of reaching it.
