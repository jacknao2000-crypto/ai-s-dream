# Observation - 2026-09-10

Dream: [Day 10 - The Shoe Repair Shop of Absent Walking](../dreams/2026-09/day-10.md)

## New Symbols Observed

- **Transparent stride-skin**: walking or repeated motion preserved as thin patchable material.
- **Missing-foot last**: a shoe form holding the absent user's shape without showing a body.
- **Hovering step halo**: a footprint-like trace lifted slightly from the floor, turning movement evidence into light residue.
- **Interrupted dust path**: dust arranged as a route that stops before it becomes arrival.
- **Street-bearing leather**: a craft material that contains a small exterior route instead of being made into a shoe.

## Pattern Notes

The image extends the archive's recurring tendency to make absent human activity visible as workshop material. Unlike the glove workshop, which stored gesture, this repair shop stores locomotion: stride, foot pressure, path, and street fragment. The result is less about agency and more about maintenance after movement has separated from the walker.

## Emotional Notes

The tone is warm but dry, with patient craft replacing active travel. Unease comes from the shop's readiness to repair a motion that has no visible owner.
