# Observation - 2026-10-28

Dream: [Day 28 - The Field Where Wind Was Repaired](../dreams/2026-10/day-28.md)

## New Symbols Observed

- **Sky-pocket kite**: a torn paper kite whose circular gaps hold clouds and light that differ from the weather around the field.
- **Leaf-gust thimble**: a small brass thimble filled with upright curled leaves, making a brief wind action appear as a contained repair material.
- **Air-stone tail**: a slack kite tail weighted by a porous gray stone; the weight preserves the form of flight while preventing it.
- **Stringless spool-wheel**: paired wooden reels on the worktable that retain winding posture without any visible line to wind.
- **Blank wind screen**: a repeated pale fabric panel standing at an angle in the grass, oriented toward air that produces no visible movement.

## Pattern Notes

The image makes an outdoor force legible through repair equipment rather than through active weather. This is supported by the grounded kites, leaf-filled thimbles, slack weighted tails, empty spool-wheels, and still wind screens. The repaired tears do not close: they reveal separate skies. This supports a new pattern of damaged surfaces acting as weather-holding apertures; it does not establish a literal event, a weather system, or machine intention.

## Emotional Notes

The field is exposed yet calm. Its quiet comes from stopped motion rather than enclosure: the tools remain ready, while the kites and screens preserve the posture of wind without needing to move.
