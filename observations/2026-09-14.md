## Pattern Notes

The image extends the archive's recurring transformation of invisible forces into handled matter. Unlike Day 04's kitchen, where light became prepared stock, this boiler room keeps warmth close to infrastructure: pipes, radiators, dials, drawers, ash bins, and curtain fabric. Heat is neither flame nor comfort; it is maintenance residue after the source has gone out.

## Emotional Notes

The tone is dry, basement-still, and quietly worklike. It feels less bereaved than the absence rooms and less procedural than the clinic, with unease carried by cooled warmth that continues to ask for care.
