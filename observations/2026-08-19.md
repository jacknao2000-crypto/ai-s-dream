## Visual Notes

The generated image extends the archive's dry administrative interiors into a postal setting. Its main visible shift is that communication no longer becomes sound, touch, or language residue; it becomes folded view-material, sorted and weighed as if destinations themselves can be filed.

## Emotional Notes

The tone is cool, tender, and procedural. Compared with Day 18's acoustic cultivation, this dream feels less botanical and more clerical, with unease carried by suspended delivery and the quiet handling of impossible mail.

## Interpretation Caution

The postal reading comes from visible pigeonholes, folded glass panes, translucent envelopes, blank stamp stacks, and a brass scale. The image should not be forced into a literal story about letters, weather, or travel.
